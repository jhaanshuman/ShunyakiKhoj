🏛️ About ShunyakiKhoj & SanskritAI

ShunyakiKhoj (शून्याकी खोज) is an authoritative research and computational platform dedicated to Indian Astronomy (Khagola Shastra), Drik Ganita Panchanga, Parashari & Jaimini Horoscopy, and Paninian Sanskrit Linguistics.

Our core mission is to bridge ancient Sanatana wisdom with modern computational precision. Rather than relying on outdated mean-motion approximation tables, ShunyakiKhoj integrates modern numerical integration algorithms (Swiss Ephemeris based on NASA JPL DE431/DE406 ephemeris) to compute planetary longitudes down to millisecond accuracy.

👨‍💻 Founder & Lead Researcher: Anshuman Jha

ShunyakiKhoj was conceived, designed, and developed by Anshuman Jha. With a background in computer science, astronomical computation, and Sanskrit grammar, Anshuman oversees the research methodology, rule engine implementations (Brihat Parasara Hora Sastra, Jaimini Sutras, Phaladeepika), and Paninian generative grammar parsers.

Core Technological Standards

  • High-Precision Ephemeris: Swiss Ephemeris algorithm matched with Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) Ayanamsha.
  • Open Research & Peer Transparency: Pre-rendered educational guides explaining every astronomical and astrological formula.
  • Zero Compromise on Authenticity: Strict adherence to classical texts without synthetic fallbacks.
