📖 How Hora is Calculated & Classical Rules

In Vedic astrology, a day is defined from local Sunrise to the next day's local Sunrise. The 24-hour cycle is divided into 12 Day Horas and 12 Night Horas.

1. The Chaldean Planetary Order

The sequence of planetary rulers follows the Chaldean order based on their apparent speeds: Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Sun → Venus → Mercury → Moon. The 1st Hora at local sunrise is always ruled by the lord of that weekday (e.g., Sunday = Sun, Monday = Moon, Tuesday = Mars, Wednesday = Mercury, Thursday = Jupiter, Friday = Venus, Saturday = Saturn).

2. Characteristics of Planetary Horas

  • Sun (Surya) Hora: Favoring administrative tasks, meeting authorities, government work, and applying for positions.
  • Moon (Chandra) Hora: Auspicious for emotional connections, public relations, gardening, creative arts, and water-related activities.
  • Mars (Mangal) Hora: Favorable for competitive sports, physical effort, land transactions, and engineering work. Avoid arguments.
  • Mercury (Budha) Hora: Excellent for commerce, accounting, coding, writing, studying, and signing agreements.
  • Jupiter (Guru) Hora: Universally auspicious for finance, higher learning, legal matters, marriage, and spiritual initiation.
  • Venus (Shukra) Hora: Favorable for arts, music, buying vehicles, jewelry, romance, and entertainment.
  • Saturn (Shani) Hora: Best suited for labor, dealing with iron/machinery, real estate maintenance, and disciplined meditation. Avoid starting new joyful events.
