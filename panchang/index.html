☀️ Complete Guide to Vedic Panchanga & Astronomical Calculation

The Vedic Panchanga (पञ्चाङ्ग) is the traditional Hindu astronomical almanac that structures time based on real-time movements of the Sun and Moon. Meaning "five limbs," the Panchanga evaluates the energetic fabric of every moment through Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, and Karana. ShunyakiKhoj provides pre-rendered research and high-precision calculations powered by NASA JPL ephemeris algorithms.

1. What is Panchanga? The Five Sacred Limbs

  • Tithi (Lunar Day): The time period during which the Moon advances 12 degrees ahead of the Sun. There are 30 Tithis in a lunar month (15 in Sukla Paksha and 15 in Krishna Paksha).
  • Vara (Solar Weekday): The 7 solar days (Ravivara to Shanivara) governed sequentially by the 7 classical planets.
  • Nakshatra (Lunar Mansion): The exact stellar constellation occupied by the Moon, dividing the 360° ecliptic into 27 equal divisions of 13°20' each.
  • Yoga (Sol-Lunar Combination): Calculated by adding the celestial longitudes of the Sun and Moon, divided into 27 equal divisions of 13°20'.
  • Karana (Half Lunar Day): Half of a Tithi (6° Moon-Sun elongation). There are 11 Karanas (7 moveable, 4 fixed).
