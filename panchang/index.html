1. What is Panchanga? The Five Sacred Limbs

In Sanatana Dharma, time is not merely a quantitative linear ticker; it is a qualitative wave governed by cosmic bodies. The five limbs (Angas) of Panchanga correspond to specific solar-lunar geometric relationships:

  • Tithi (Lunar Day): The time period during which the Moon advances 12 degrees ahead of the Sun. There are 30 Tithis in a lunar month (15 in Sukla Paksha / Waxing and 15 in Krishna Paksha / Waning).
  • Vara (Solar Weekday): The 7 solar days (Ravivara to Shanivara) governed sequentially by the 7 classical planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn).
  • Nakshatra (Lunar Mansion): The exact stellar constellation occupied by the Moon, dividing the 360° ecliptic into 27 equal divisions of 13°20' each.
  • Yoga (Sol-Lunar Combination): Calculated by adding the celestial longitudes of the Sun and Moon, divided into 27 equal divisions of 13°20' (from Vishkumbha to Vaidhriti).
  • Karana (Half Lunar Day): Half of a Tithi (6° Moon-Sun elongation). There are 11 Karanas: 7 moveable (Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Gara, Vanija, Vishti) and 4 fixed (Shakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kintughna).

2. Drik Ganita vs. Surya Siddhanta Astronomy

Historically, Panchanga calculations relied on mean planetary motion constants codified in classical texts like Surya Siddhanta. However, because planetary orbits are elliptical and subject to gravitational perturbations, calculated positions drifted from observed sky positions over centuries.

In modern times, scholars like Bal Gangadhar Tilak established Drik Ganita (observation-proven astronomy). 4. Rahu Kaal, Choghadiya and Auspicious Muhurtas

Auspicious timing (Muhurta) selection requires identifying favorable windows while steering clear of unfavorable planetary shadow periods:

Rahu Kaal: An 90-minute daily window governed by Rahu. Important commercial transactions, travel, and religious rites are avoided during Rahu Kaal.

Abhijit Muhurta: The 8th Muhurta of the day (centered precisely around local Solar Noon). It is universally auspicious and neutralizes minor astrological doshas.

Choghadiya: Divides day and night into 7 recurring 1.5-hour windows (Amrit, Shubh, Labh, Chara, Roga, Kaal, Udveg). Amrit, Shubh, and Labh are favorable, while Roga, Kaal, and Udveg are avoided.
