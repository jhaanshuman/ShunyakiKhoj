📆 Understanding Maasik Panchang & Hindu Calendar Months

The Maasik Panchang (मासिक पञ्चाङ्ग) tracks the complete 30-day lunar cycle across 12 traditional months (Chaitra to Phalguna). It is evaluated in two classical calendar systems:

  • Purnimanta System: Month begins on the day after Purnima (Full Moon). Followed predominantly in North India.
  • Amanta System: Month begins on the day after Amavasya (New Moon). Followed in Maharashtra, Gujarat, South India.
