🪔 What is Panchang? (आज का पंचांग)

Panchang (Sanskrit: पञ्चाङ्ग) literally means "five limbs" of the Vedic almanac. These five sacred elements — Tithi (lunar day), Nakshatra (lunar mansion), Yoga (auspicious combination), Karana (half-day division), and Vara (weekday) — together govern the quality of every moment in Vedic time. ShunyakiKhoj generates today's Panchang with high-precision Drik Ganita (astronomical observation method) calculations using the Swiss Ephemeris engine, providing accurate results for any city worldwide — from New Delhi to New York, Mumbai to Melbourne.

Every morning, devout Hindus consult the Panchang to determine auspicious timings for prayers, business decisions, travel, marriages, and sacred ceremonies. Our Aaj Ka Panchang (today's Panchang) section provides real-time Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda Kaal, Gulika Kaal, Brahma Muhurta, and Abhijit Muhurta — all dynamically calculated for your chosen city.

🔮 Free Kundli (Janma Kundali) Online

A Kundli or Janma Kundali (birth chart) is the foundation of Vedic Jyotish (astrology). It maps the precise positions of all nine Grahas (planets) — Surya (Sun), Chandra (Moon), Mangal (Mars), Budha (Mercury), Guru (Jupiter), Shukra (Venus), Shani (Saturn), Rahu and Ketu — at the exact moment and place of an individual's birth. ShunyakiKhoj's free Kundli generator produces complete Lagna Chart (D1), Navamsha (D9), and all Varga charts from D1 to D60, along with Vimshottari Dasha periods, Gochar (transit) analysis, and Ashtakavarga scores.

Our AI-powered Kundli system applies authentic Parashari Jyotish principles to analyse planetary strengths (Shadbala), house lordships, Yogas (Raj Yoga, Dhana Yoga, Vipareet Raja Yoga), and detailed life predictions across all twelve houses — providing the depth of a professional astrologer consultation, entirely free.

📅 Maasik Panchang – Monthly Vedic Calendar

The Maasik Panchang (monthly calendar) is an essential tool for planning the entire month according to Vedic principles. Every date tile in our interactive monthly calendar shows the Tithi, Nakshatra, major festivals, Upavas (fasting) days, and auspicious Muhurtas. Festivals like Ekadashi, Pradosh, Purnima, Amavasya, Navratri, Diwali, Holi, Ganesh Chaturthi, Janmashtami, and hundreds of regional observances are automatically highlighted. The Maasik Panchang is dynamically generated for any chosen city and month, reflecting local Sunrise times that affect Tithi calculations.

⏰ Muhurta – Auspicious Time Selection

Muhurta (मुहूर्त) is the ancient Vedic science of selecting the most auspicious moment for important life events. ShunyakiKhoj calculates multiple categories of Muhurta daily:

  • Brahma Muhurta — the sacred 48-minute window before Sunrise, ideal for meditation and study
  • Abhijit Muhurta — the powerful midday window, auspicious for most activities
  • Udaya Lagna Muhurta — rising ascendant windows throughout the day
  • Panchaka Rahita Muhurta — periods free from the inauspicious Panchaka dosha
  • Vivah Muhurta — auspicious dates and times for marriage ceremonies
  • Griha Pravesh Muhurta — for housewarming and property entry

Our Rashifal (राशिफल) provides daily, weekly, and monthly horoscope predictions for all twelve Vedic Rashis — Mesh (Aries), Vrishabha (Taurus), Mithuna (Gemini), Karka (Cancer), Simha (Leo), Kanya (Virgo), Tula (Libra), Vrischika (Scorpio), Dhanu (Sagittarius), Makara (Capricorn), Kumbha (Aquarius), and Meena (Pisces).
