A Janma Kundali (birth chart) is a two-dimensional map of the celestial sky captured at the exact moment and geographic location of an individual's birth. Based on Parashari and Jaimini Vedic astrology, the Kundli reveals natal planetary strengths, dasha timelines, karmic predispositions, and life events across 12 Bhavas (houses).
