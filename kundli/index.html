1. The 12 Bhavas (Houses) and Life Domains

The 360° zodiac is divided into 12 equal 30-degree houses starting from the Lagna (Ascendant)—the sign rising on the eastern horizon at birth:

  • 1st House (Tanu Bhava): Physical appearance, self-identity, health, longevity, and personality.
  • 2nd House (Dhana Bhava): Wealth, speech, family inheritance, assets, and early education.
  • 3rd House (Sahaja Bhava): Younger siblings, courage, communication, short journeys, and fine arts.
  • 4th House (Sukha Bhava): Mother, real estate, vehicles, happiness, domestic environment, and fixed assets.
  • 5th House (Putra Bhava): Children, intelligence, purva punya (past merit), romance, speculation, and mantra siddhi.
  • 6th House (Ari Bhava): Enemies, diseases, debts, litigation, service, and competitive exams.
  • 7th House (Yuvati Bhava): Marriage partner, business partnerships, public relations, and foreign trade.
  • 8th House (Randhra Bhava): Longevity, sudden inheritance, occult research, transformation, and chronic illness.
  • 9th House (Dharma Bhava): Father, guru, higher wisdom, pilgrimage, luck, and spiritual righteousness.
  • 10th House (Karma Bhava): Profession, career, social status, government honors, and authority.
  • 11th House (Labha Bhava): Gains, elder siblings, fulfillment of desires, social network, and income.
  • 12th House (Vyaya Bhava): Expenses, foreign lands, moksha (liberation), hospitals, and subconscious mind.

2. The 16 Varga Division Charts (Shodashavarga)

In Vedic astrology, looking at the main D1 (Rashi) chart alone is insufficient. Maharishi Parashara specified 16 harmonic divisional charts (Vargas) to micro-analyze specific facets of destiny:

D9 Navamsha Chart: The most vital divisional chart. It reveals inner spiritual potential, marriage quality, spouse characteristics, and confirms whether a strong planet in D1 retains real strength.

D10 Dashamsha Chart: Micro-analyzes career achievements, executive power, professional promotions, and public fame.

D60 Shashtiamsha Chart: Divides each sign into 60 micro-parts of 0°30' each, unveiling deep past-life karmic roots and subtle individual differences between twins born minutes apart.

3. Vimshottari Dasha: The 120-Year Planetary Cycle

Events in Vedic horoscopy do not occur at random; they blossom during the active operating period (Dasha) of the responsible planet. Under the 120-year Vimshottari Dasha system, the starting dasha is governed by the Moon's exact birth Nakshatra. Each Major Period (Mahadasha) is subdivided into Sub-Periods (Antardasha), Pratyantardasha, Sookshmadasha, and Pranadasha, pinpointing exact event timing windows.
