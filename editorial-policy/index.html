📜 Editorial Policy & Research Standards

ShunyakiKhoj adheres to rigorous research standards in publishing educational material on Indian Astronomy, Vedic Horoscopy, and Sanskrit Linguistics.

1. Primary Source Citations

All astrological rules and divisional chart interpretations are derived directly from authoritative classical Sanskrit treatises, including Brihat Parasara Hora Sastra (BPHS), Jaimini Sutras, Phaladeepika by Mantreswara, Saravali by Kalyana Varma, and Panini's Ashtadhyayi.

2. Astronomical Precision & Verification

Our Drik Ganita algorithms use Swiss Ephemeris data matched against NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) DE431/DE406 ephemerides. All festival and Tithi boundary calculations are verified to reflect real physical sky observations.
