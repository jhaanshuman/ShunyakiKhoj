🪔 The Foundations of Sanatana Dharma

Sanatana Dharma (The Eternal Way) is built upon four fundamental Vedas (Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda), 108 Upanishads exploring Brahman and Atman, 18 Mahapuranas detailing cosmos and avatars, and the 6 Darshanas (philosophical systems including Vedanta and Yoga).
