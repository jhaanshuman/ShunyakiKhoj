1. The 8 Kootas & 36 Points Breakdown

  • 1. Varna (1 Point): Spiritual compatibility and work orientation (Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra).
  • 2. Vashya (2 Points): Mutual attraction, influence, and control in the relationship.
  • 3. Tara (3 Points): Health, longevity, and fortune derived from natal Nakshatra distances.
  • 4. Yoni (4 Points): Physical, intimate, and biological compatibility symbolized by animal totems.
  • 5. Graha Maitri (5 Points): Mental rapport, friendship, and emotional understanding between Moon lords.
  • 6. Gana (6 Points): Temperament and lifestyle alignment (Deva / Divine, Manushya / Human, Rakshasa / Fiery).
  • 7. Bhakoot (7 Points): Emotional bonding, financial prosperity, and family growth based on Moon sign distances (2-12, 5-9, 6-8 positions).
  • 8. Nadi (8 Points): Physiological, genetic, and health compatibility for offspring (Ata, Madhya, Antya Nadi).

2. Scoring Thresholds & Classification

A total score out of 36 points determines compatibility:

  • Below 18 Gunas: Not recommended without remedy (High incompatibility).
  • 18 to 24 Gunas: Average compatibility (Acceptable with mutual maturity).
  • 25 to 32 Gunas: Excellent compatibility (Highly recommended for long-term harmony).
  • 33 to 36 Gunas: Outstanding compatibility (Rare cosmic match).

3. Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha) Exceptions

Mangal Dosha occurs when Mars occupies the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house from Lagna, Moon, or Venus. However, Maharishi Parashara listed key cancellation rules: 1) If both partners have Mangal Dosha, the dosha neutralizes. 2) Mars in own sign (Aries/Scorpio) or exaltation sign (Capricorn) cancels dosha. 3) Jupiter's aspect on Mars cancels dosha.
